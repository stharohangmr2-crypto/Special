      How to play

      • Be the last banana standing. Blast crates, dodge flames, trap your friends.
      • Move: drag the left side of the screen, or use WASD / arrow keys.
      • Bomb: the Bomb button or Space. Bombs explode after 2.5 seconds in a cross shape. Walls stop the blast, crates break it.
      • Red tiles show where flames are about to land. Bombs chain into each other.
      • Power-ups from crates: extra bomb, bigger blast, speed boost, shield (blocks one hit), remote detonator (Boom button or E), banana peel trap (Peel button or Q), bomb kick (walk into a bomb to push it).
      • Matches last up to 3 minutes. If time runs out with several players alive, it is a draw.
      • If you lose connection mid-match you have 20 seconds to come back before you are knocked out.
